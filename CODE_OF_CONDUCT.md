# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or public/private harassment.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
